Our Mission

To provide everyday people with accessible energy solutions, solar generators, and reliable blackout protection.

Why Do We Exist?

Argentines suffer from constant power outages and voltage drops. Based on our personal research using ENRE data:

  • Between 300,000 and 600,000 users are simultaneously without power on the worst days.
  • Approximately 1 to 2 million unique users suffered at least one outage in 2025 in the AMBA region alone.
  • Tens of thousands of interruptions occur annually, counting micro-cuts, partial, and total blackouts.

Local Solar Potential

The Tropic of Capricorn is the closest tropic, located at about 23° 26' South latitude. Here, the highest number of low-income individuals, associations, and NGOs can heavily benefit from affordable solar products and batteries due to excellent solar irradiance.
